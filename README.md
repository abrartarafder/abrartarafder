## About Me

I'm a recent **Honours Bachelor of Science in Computer Science graduate from York University (Lassonde School of Engineering)**, with nearly four years of professional experience through co-op, internship, and contract opportunities.

My background spans **software development, database engineering, data analytics, DevOps, automation, and web development**. I've worked across utility operations, academic research, and technology-focused organizations, developing solutions that improve efficiency, streamline workflows, and solve real-world problems.

My professional experience includes:
- **Hydro One:** Automated operational workflows, developed GitLab CI/CD pipelines, and supported database release processes within OT Operations.
- **York University:** Developed PostgreSQL databases and Python ETL pipelines to process and standardize 10,000+ historical records.
- **WalkWithWeb:** Built responsive web applications, designed Figma prototypes, and supported digital research and analytics initiatives.
- **Opal LLC:** Contributed to frontend development, application prototyping, and digital marketing initiatives.

I'm currently exploring opportunities in **AI Development, Software Development, Data Engineering, Database Development, Business/Data Analytics, and Technical Project roles**.

Outside of technology, I'm also passionate about drone photography, travel, and exploring new perspectives through aerial imagery.
